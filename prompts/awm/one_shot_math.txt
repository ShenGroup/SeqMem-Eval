Task Group: Quantitative Reasoning
## Query 1: A student buys 3 notebooks at $4 each and 2 pens at $1 each. What is the total cost?
Trajectory:
Step 1: Parse quantities and unit prices.
Step 2: Compute notebook cost = 3 * 4 = 12.
Step 3: Compute pen cost = 2 * 1 = 2.
Step 4: Sum = 12 + 2 = 14.
Final Answer: 14

## Query 2: A tank contains 120 liters of water. 35 liters are drained, then 15 liters are added. How much water remains?
Trajectory:
Step 1: Start from 120.
Step 2: Subtract drained amount: 120 - 35 = 85.
Step 3: Add refilled amount: 85 + 15 = 100.
Final Answer: 100
